# Glossary

- **Installation:** One deployed instance of Postaal.
- **User:** A person authenticated to an installation.
- **Social account:** A provider account connected through authorization.
- **Platform:** A social network/provider such as Instagram or LinkedIn.
- **Post:** The shared content composition.
- **Post target:** One destination account selected for a post.
- **Master content:** The base caption shared across destinations unless overridden.
- **Platform override:** Destination-specific caption or content variation.
- **Preview:** A simulated rendering of how content may appear on a platform.
- **Publishing job:** Durable background work to publish to one destination.
- **Queue:** The ordered set of pending or scheduled background jobs.
- **Provider adapter:** Integration boundary around a social platform API.
- **Partial publication:** At least one destination succeeded and at least one failed.
- **UTC:** Canonical timezone for persisted scheduled instants.
